# CakeCraft

CakeCraft is a cake customization website that allows users to browse and choose from pre-designed cakes based on their preferences. The platform is designed to make cake selection easier, more visual, and more convenient for customers who want beautifully crafted cakes for different occasions.

## Overview

CakeCraft provides a simple and user-friendly interface where customers can explore available cake designs, view cake details, and select a cake that matches their preferred style, theme, or celebration. It is ideal for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, and other special events.

## Features

- Browse pre-designed cake options
- View cake details and design previews
- Choose cakes based on preferences
- User-friendly and responsive interface
- Organized cake categories for easier selection
- Designed for a smooth customer experience

## Purpose

The purpose of CakeCraft is to help customers quickly find cake designs that match their needs without starting from scratch. By offering pre-designed cake options, the website simplifies the cake selection process while still giving users a personalized experience.
